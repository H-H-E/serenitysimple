02 / Applications

Remote power applications

Close to the fuel.
Close to the work.

Remote oil and gas operations are the first focus. Larger systems could serve other industrial applications over time.

Each opportunity depends on fuel quality, power demand, access and operating conditions.

01Oil & gas operationsInitial focus

First application: field monitoring and instrumentation. Assess the available gas, electrical load and operating conditions for each site.

Discuss a potential site
View a field-site illustration

Longer-term opportunities · subject to development

02Utilities & microgridsLonger-term opportunity

Distributed power for locations beyond or at the edge of the grid. Integration and site operating requirements would shape each potential project.

03Data centres & cloudLonger-term opportunity

A longer-term application for larger systems. Power quality, continuity and scale would need validation against the facility’s requirements.

04Mining & industryLonger-term opportunity

Consider operations where suitable fuel and electrical demand coincide. Site access, environment and maintenance planning belong in the first conversation.

05Rental power providersLonger-term opportunity

A potential route for temporary or distributed power as the platform develops. Transport, setup and duty-cycle requirements would need evaluation.
