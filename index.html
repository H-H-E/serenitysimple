Technology

One unit on the gas line. Nothing else to install.

A fuel cell turns gas into electricity without burning it. Ours needs no water supply or equipment room, runs as quietly as a conversation (<40 dB), and fits where the generator sits today.

Inside the MS-SOFC stack Emissions & grant calculator

The problem

Most of the fuel burned at remote sites becomes heat.

97%

Thermoelectric generators

Lost as heat in the generators that power instruments and cabinets today (~3% electrical efficiency).

69%

Generator sets

Lost as heat in the diesel and gas gensets that power larger sites, alongside heavy fuel transport logistics and particulates.

Sources: Gentherm Global Power TEG datasheets; Cummins 20 to 100 kW gensets.

Breakthrough Cell Architecture

Why Metal-Supported SOFCs change field deployment.

Conventional ceramic fuel cells are fragile, take 3+ hours to warm up, and require 20 m³ of external water treatment and steam reforming plant. Serenity's porous stainless-steel substrate and internal catalyst solve all three.

01 · Porous Metal Support Substrate <15 Min Start

High-permeability stainless steel replaces brittle ceramic supports, surviving rapid thermal cycling and mechanical vibration in remote field transport.

02 · Internal Catalytic Reforming 0 External Water

Reforms pipeline natural gas, raw wellhead gas, biogas, or hydrogen blends directly inside the stack—eliminating external steam reformers and water deionisers.

03 · Thin-Film Dense Electrolyte Up to 60% Target Eff.

Electrochemically oxidizes fuel without combustion flame, cutting fuel consumption up to 8x vs. TEGs and eliminating NOx, SOx, and particulate emissions.

04 · Compact Modular Enclosure 1 m³ vs 20 m³

Shrinks reference balance-of-plant volume by up to 20x, dropping directly into existing wellsite cabinets, PRMS stations, and mobile skids.
